# Field guide

One page for community workers using the VacantWatch field app.

## Set up (once)

1. On your phone, open the link your coordinator sent (it ends in `/field/`).
2. Sign in with the temporary password, then choose your own.
3. Browser menu → **Add to Home Screen**. It now opens like an app and works without signal.
4. Allow **location** when asked. It sorts properties by distance and pins what you report.

## On the ground

- **Stay on the street.** Never go into a building, garden or yard. Never force or try a door.
- **People first.** If you see signs someone may be sleeping there, tick *Signs of rough sleeping*. The app shows the outreach contact; refer to StreetLink or your outreach team today. If someone is in danger, call 999.
- **Photos of the building only.** No people, no number plates, no looking into windows.
- **Notes about the building, not people.** No names or descriptions.

## Using the app

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| See what is nearby | **Near me**. Coloured bars show priority (red is most urgent). |
| Record a visit | Open the property → **Log a visit** → tap its status, condition, security and everything you can see → photos → **Save**. |
| Report a new empty property | **Report** → address → **Nearest** fills the postcode from your location (check it against a sign) → tick what you see → **Save property**. If it is already on the register, your visit is added to the existing record. |
| Find help for someone | **Help nearby** lists shelters, food banks, outreach and health services with phone numbers and directions. |
| See your jobs | **Tasks** → *Started* or *Done*. |

## No signal?

Keep going. Everything you save is kept on the phone and sent automatically when you are back online; the bar at the top shows how many items are waiting. If something says *need attention*, open it to retry or discard. Do not uninstall the app or clear its data while items are waiting.

## Which status?

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| Suspected vacant | Looks empty but you are not sure. |
| Confirmed vacant | Clearly empty: boarded, mail piling up, no furniture, or confirmed by a neighbour or the council. |
| Derelict | Empty and in poor repair: damage, broken windows, overgrown. |
| Abandoned | Left to decay with no sign of an owner taking care of it. |
| Occupied | Someone lives there now. |
| Brought back into use | Was empty, now lived in again. Good news: record it. |
